Orientation

What Tyme Hall is, how to approach it, and what not to assume.

A public threshold, not an authority source

Tyme Hall is an experimental public read-surface for a larger body of open research, software, records, hypotheses and collaborative work called Sovereign Intelligence. The Hall exists to make that body inspectable without requiring prior knowledge of its internal vocabulary.

Five public relationships

  • Office reports approved present institutional state.
  • Scrolls preserve reviewed public inheritance.
  • Laboratory exposes bounded active experimentation without granting Canon authority.
  • Constellation helps navigate retained relationships without becoming a second source of truth.
  • Contribution provides governed paths to challenge, correct, reproduce or participate without automatically granting authority.

How to read the Hall

Distinguish what is established from what is proposed. Canon records durable institutional definitions or decisions. Hypotheses remain testable claims. Experiments report bounded attempts and observations. Interpretations preserve meaning without becoming empirical proof. Archive preserves lineage without implying current authority.

The deeper vocabulary

Beneath the public doorway, the Hall exposes older and more technical system terms including Codex, AVOT, TYME and QIL. These describe different parts of the institutional machinery. Use the Constellation and public source records to reconstruct them; do not invent capabilities merely because a term exists.

Start without belief

You do not need to accept the project's premises to inspect it. Follow sources, examine repositories, challenge claims, reproduce experiments where appropriate, compare human and machine-readable state, and preserve uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.
