Orientation

What Tyme Hall is, and how to approach it.

A public threshold

Tyme Hall is an experimental public interface for a larger body of open research, software, records, hypotheses and collaborative work called Sovereign Intelligence. The Hall exists to make that body navigable without requiring prior knowledge of its internal vocabulary.

How to read the Hall

Distinguish what is established from what is proposed. Canon records durable definitions and decisions. Hypotheses remain testable claims. Trails show paths through the work. State describes what is actually active now.

Start without belief

You do not need to accept the project's premises to inspect it. Follow sources, examine repositories, challenge claims, reproduce experiments where appropriate, and preserve uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.
