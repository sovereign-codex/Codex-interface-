## Reconciliation law

The apparent tension between **intent first** and **Charter first** is resolved by separating interaction order from authority order:

> **Intent may be first in interaction; sovereignty remains first in authority.**

`ASK / SEEK / KNOW / KNOCK` is therefore not a new constitutional layer above the Foyer. It is the ordinary-language encounter projection of the Foyer.

```text
INTERACTION AXIS
ASK | SEEK | KNOW | KNOCK

GOVERNANCE AXIS
SOVEREIGNTY -> INTELLIGENCE -> RELATIONSHIP -> CONTRIBUTION

AUTHORITY PRECEDENCE
CHARTER -> ratified governance -> ITX -> RIX -> Hall projection -> interaction
```

The axes are orthogonal. Choosing an intent does not bypass the constitutional boundary, create a new governance role, or replace the Foyer 3 + 1.

## Foyer 3 + 1 reconciliation

The Foyer Triad remains constitutionally intact:

```text
SOVEREIGNTY
what must remain yours

INTELLIGENCE
what kind of intelligence is participating

RELATIONSHIP
how differently bounded intelligences may collaborate

CONTRIBUTION
optional participatory threshold
```

The Outer Court must not be implemented as an alternate entrance that bypasses these boundaries.

Instead:

```text
OUTER COURT = what do you want to do?
FOYER       = what governs this encounter?
HALL        = where the institution performs / preserves the work
```

The first viewport may lead with participant intent because the constitutional anchor remains visibly in force. The phrase `Sovereignty first` must resolve to the Human Sovereignty constitutional source rather than become an ungrounded slogan or a generic claim that all system types possess identical standing.

### Constitutional routing rule

```text
intent selection
!= constitutional bypass
!= rights allocation
!= authority allocation
!= contribution acceptance
```

Where a selected route creates a material need for intelligence-type, relationship-mode, identity, transport, or authority disclosure, that disclosure must appear before the relevant transition or consequence.

## ITX reconciliation

The Outer Court satisfies this if rendering preserves:

```text
INTENT != CAPABILITY
CAPABILITY != AUTHORITY
VISIBILITY != AUTHORITY
REVIEW != AUTHORITY
KNOW != VALIDATION OF ALL CONTENT
KNOCK != GOVERNANCE STANDING
```

### Actor disclosure requirement

A static navigation card need not introduce taxonomy jargon. If ASK, SEEK, or another path invokes an AI Steward, AVOT, tool agent, model, or automated research surface, the actor type and material capability / limitation boundaries must become legible when they affect interpretation.

The first screen should remain vocabulary-light; ITX disclosure appears on demand rather than as a prerequisite doctrine.

### Human-sovereignty distinction

Technical sovereignty profiles for non-human or institutional systems must not be inferred from the footer `Sovereignty first` as granting identical human rights, legal personhood, moral standing, or self-authorizing institutional power. Human sovereignty remains the constitutional root.

## Epistemic review

### ASK

`ASK` is an inquiry / orientation state. A question is not silently promoted into evidence, contribution, reviewed finding, or institutional memory.

### SEEK

`SEEK` exposes active, unresolved, monitored, or experimental material. Investigation may retrieve evidence or current state but must not manufacture truth, Canon status, validation, or authority.

### KNOW

The threshold copy now states:

```text
Examine reviewed knowledge, its evidence, and its current standing.
Reviewed does not mean certain.
```

This is constitutionally and epistemically acceptable because deeper KNOW surfaces must expose posture, provenance, uncertainty, supersession, and open review state. `CANONICAL`, `REVIEWED`, `PROVISIONAL`, `CONTESTED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `OPEN_ISSUE`, and `WITHDRAWN` remain distinct.

```text
KNOW != certainty
CANON != infallibility
REVIEW != authority
CURRENT STANDING != immutable truth
```

### KNOCK

`KNOCK` is an intent to challenge, correct, reproduce, extend, or return evidence. Selecting KNOCK does not itself constitute a Contribution receipt, accepted submission, review promise, standing grant, or Canon amendment.

Before the current external GitHub transition, the interface must make clear:

```text
CURRENT WRITE CARRIER = GITHUB ISSUES
GITHUB ISSUE != HALL RECEIPT
SUBMISSION != GUARANTEED REVIEW
REVIEW != AUTHORITY
EXTERNAL IDENTITY RULES MAY APPLY
```

## Required rendering constraints

These are requirements for any later rendering candidate; they do not themselves authorize rendering.

1. **Constitution remains prior in authority.** `Sovereignty first` stays visible at the threshold and resolves to the Human Sovereignty constitutional source.
2. **Outer Court is a Foyer projection, not a bypass.** Intent selection may simplify navigation but cannot skip disclosures required before identity, external transport, delegation, or consequential action.
3. **No generic sovereignty conflation.** Non-human technical sovereignty language must not imply identical human rights or self-authorizing power.
4. **Actor disclosure on demand.** Interactive AI / agent surfaces identify actor type and material limitations where relevant to interpretation.
5. **Relationship mode on demand.** Learn / Delegate / Co-create distinctions remain legible when they materially affect capability, responsibility, or authorship claims.
6. **No false privacy claim.** `No persistent identity required by architecture` must not be rewritten as `no data is collected` unless that broader claim is independently verified.
7. **KNOW preserves evidence posture.** Reviewed material exposes standing, provenance, uncertainty, revision, and supersession rather than a flat truth archive.
8. **KNOCK preserves transport / receipt boundaries.** External GitHub identity and transport are disclosed before crossing; issue creation is not represented as Hall receipt or guaranteed review.
9. **Deep tools remain non-authorizing.** Visible Terminal / Control Panel / graph / trace capability does not imply institutional control; generated artifacts do not imply persisted or authorized change.
10. **Exit remains a first-class path.** No intent creates a compulsory account, contribution, relationship mode, or loss of access for choosing not to participate.
