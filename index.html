COLD-START REVIEW · CYCLE 02

Enter without prior context.

Tyme Hall is the public read-surface of a developing body of research, software, institutional memory and human–AI collaboration. It is designed so the work can be inspected before its internal vocabulary has to be learned.

No belief is required. Follow evidence, inspect provenance, preserve uncertainty, distinguish mature inheritance from active experimentation, and report contradictions rather than resolving them by assumption.

Five ways to enter the living institution

These are different relationships to institutional knowledge, not five equivalent content categories.

Office

Present state: what is active now, what changed, what is next, what is waiting, and what requires review.

Scrolls

Inherited canon and reviewed public artifacts that crossed an explicit publication boundary.

Laboratory

Active becoming: hypotheses, experiments, prototypes and bounded work that must not silently acquire Canon authority.

Constellation

Relational navigation across public systems, work, evidence and inheritance.

Contribution

Challenge, reproduce, document, build or correct without confusing participation with authority.
