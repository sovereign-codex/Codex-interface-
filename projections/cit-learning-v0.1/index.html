This is not the live Tyme Hall. It is a review candidate derived from a validated Tyme-Lab rehearsal. Nothing on this surface is submitted to the Hall, no account is created, and no participant is ranked.

Start with something you want to understand

You do not need to know TYME vocabulary first. Choose a sample question, then move among speaking, seeing, trying, making and optionally sharing. The question stays the same while the representation changes.

How should assistance relate to this encounter?

You control this relationship. Changing a view or renderer does not silently change it.

What this candidate is testing

The same semantic encounter should remain coherent across audio, text, interactive and making surfaces. Assistance mode must stay explicit. A successful action must not silently become a capability claim. Contribution must remain optional and pre-institutional.
