Canon

What the system currently treats as durable orientation.

Canonical ≠ unquestionable

Canon is the current shared reference layer: stable definitions, architecture, governance rules and accepted decisions. It can change through explicit revision and provenance; it is not a claim of infallibility.

Core distinctions

  • Canon: adopted system knowledge or decisions.
  • Hypothesis: proposition awaiting sufficient validation.
  • Experiment: a procedure and its observations.
  • Interpretation: meaning inferred from evidence or experience.
  • Archive: preserved material that may not represent current doctrine.
